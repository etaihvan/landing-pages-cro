[Spade's merchant graph]

Built on a record of every business, and how they relate

Every transaction is matched against it, including the long-tail, local, new, and third-party-routed charges other approaches miss.

01The long tail, down to the address
Small, local, and new businesses other approaches miss, with the store's address.
02The business behind the third party
The verified merchant behind a delivery app, a processor, an app store, or a marketplace, with the third party named alongside it.
03Every location, rolled up to its brand
Every location connects to its brand, and every brand to its owner, so a reward or control set on a brand applies across its locations.
04Kept current as merchants change
Merchant IDs (MIDs) rotate, processors change, businesses change hands, and a storefront can reopen on the same terminal as something else. Spade keeps pace, so the match holds.
05One merchant across every rail
Card, ACH, wire, RTP, ATM, checks, and internal transfers resolve to the same verified merchant, under one counterparty ID. Your customers see the same business however they paid, and your teams see their full money movement in one picture.
